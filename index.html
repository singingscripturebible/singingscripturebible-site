God's Word, carried on a melody

Our mission is to carry God's Word to the world through song, one passage at a time, until the whole Bible has been set to music.

Each song is a passage of Scripture set closely to the words of the King James Bible, so it can be sung, remembered, and carried with you through the day.

A simple hope

Singing Scripture began with a simple hope: that God's Word would walk beside us through each day.

Each passage is chosen and shaped with care, and set to music with the help of modern music tools. New passages are added as each one is finished, working through the Bible a little at a time.

Each time these songs are played, God's Word reaches a little further, and that encourages us to keep bringing it into song.
