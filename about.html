Our story

A ministry project from Oshkosh, Wisconsin, putting Scripture to song.

✦

How it began

Singing Scripture began with a simple hope: that God's Word would walk beside us through each day.

From that hope came a plan: to move through Scripture passage by passage and give each one a melody, so that the words of the King James Bible could be sung at home, in the car, at church and at school. The work began in 2026 in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, and it continues one passage at a time.

Our mission

Our mission is to carry God's Word to the world through song, one passage at a time, until the whole Bible has been set to music.

Each time these songs are played, God's Word reaches a little further, and that encourages us to keep bringing it into song.

✦

How the songs are made

The passage

Each song begins with a passage of Scripture. The words are kept close to the King James Bible, so a listener who knows the passage will recognize it. Where a word must bend to fit the melody, it stays a close paraphrase of the King James text.

The music

Each passage is chosen and shaped with care, and set to music with the help of modern music tools. The melodies and voices are created with the help of AI music tools, and the painted artwork with AI image tools.

The video

Every song is shared on our YouTube channel with painted artwork for the passage and the words on screen, so it can be followed, sung along with, and remembered.

Who we are

Singing Scripture is a ministry project of a Wisconsin Christian father, based in Oshkosh, Wisconsin. The songs are made for churches and pastors, homeschool families, Christian schools and music teachers, retirement communities, chaplains, and shelters, and for anyone who would like to carry God's Word through the day.
